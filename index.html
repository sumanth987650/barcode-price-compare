Barcode Price Compare

Scan or enter a product barcode (GTIN/UPC) and instantly see its current Walmart price and product details.

How it works

1

Enter a barcode

Type or scan any 12-digit UPC or 14-digit GTIN from a product package.

2

We look it up

The tool finds the matching product in the Walmart.com catalog.

3

See the price

View the current price, product name and image, with a link to buy on Walmart.com.

Example

Barcode 812154035049 returns:

ProductNative Body Wash, Sea Salt & Cedar, 18 fl oz
GTIN00812154035049
RetailerWalmart.com

Who it's for

Shoppers who want to quickly check what a product costs at Walmart before they buy, whether they're in another store or comparing options at home.
